# Working with Vulnerable Populations
## Research & Learn
### Who are considered vulnerable populations, and what challenges might they face in digital spaces?
- I think the vulnerable are children, elders, and people with disability. They may face challenges like
  online harassment, having a hard time to understand the content, and privacy risks.
### What ethical considerations are important when designing or working with neurodivergent individuals?
- The design must consider their different needs, protect privacy, ensure the accessibility, and provide flexible
  ways on how they can interact with the content.
### How can you make interactions and content more accessible for people with ADHD or Autism?
- Use clear and simple language, structured layouts, and consistent design.
  Minimize distractions, provide instructions, and allow customization
### How can we support neurodivergent team members in a professional setting? 
- Support can include clear communication, flexible work styles, structured tasks, and a respectful environment.

## Reflection
### How can you adjust your communication style to be more inclusive of neurodivergent users and teammates?
- I can use clear, simple, direct language, and give structured instructions.
  I will also be patient and allow time for their response.
### What are some common UX or communication pitfalls that might make Focus Bear less accessible or supportive?
- Common UX or communication pitfalls that might make Focus Bear less accessible include inconsistent design, overly complex interfaces, too many distractions, and unclear instructions. These issues can confuse users and make it harder for neurodivergent individuals to stay focused and complete tasks effectively.
### What is one practical change you can make in your work to better support vulnerable populations?
- By ensuring by work uses clear and yet simple language with well-structured content. So that users
  find it easier to understand and navigate.
