# Professionalism at Focus Bear

## Research & Learn
### What are examples of professional vs. unprofessional behaviour in a casual work environment?
- Professional behaviour in a casual work environment includes being responsible,
 focused on tasks, and communicating respectfully in meetings. Unprofessional behaviour includes being late, using inappropriate language,
 and not taking responsibility for tasks.

### What does respectful communication look like in remote teams? (e.g., tone in messages, handling disagreements, responding to feedback)
- Respectful communication in remote teams means handling situations calmly and using clear, professional, and considerate language. Even during hard times, when facing challenges, or
  disagreements, team members communicate properly, listen to each other's perspectives, and
  avoid misunderstandings by being patient.

### How can you give and receive feedback professionally? 
- I always think first on how should I say my thoughts without hurting someone's feelings.
  However, when someone give their feedbacks about me, I didn't take it personally, I always tell to my self that's for my growth.

## Reflection
### Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?
- Yes, I’ve witnessed it in school during group projects. For example, in one project, everyone had a clear role, we communicated regularly, and each member completed their tasks on time. We also helped each other when someone was struggling. Because of this, we finished early and produced better results. This showed me that having a shared goal, clear communication, and responsibility leads to effective teamwork and professionalism.

### What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?
- I make sure to use simple and understandable words, listen to other perspectives, and avoid interrupting. I also try to be mindful of tone, respect different viewpoints, and communicate in a way that is inclusive and easy for everyone to understand.

### How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?
- By staying respecful to my teammates, being supportive to them, and responsible on my tasks. Also by keeping positive mindset.

### How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?
- I must prepare my questions if I have one, by reviewing the meeting agenda before it starts. and being ready to update my progress.

### What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?
- When looking a way to contribute to the team, by asking questions, seeking feedbacks, and taking initiate.

### How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?
- I would follow up by politely sending emails/messages, clearly stating what I need.

* Ex: 
  Hi John,
  Just following up on this. I need (the information/action) to proceed with (my task).
  Please let me know when you have a chance, Thanks!.
  Best regards,
  Clarence

- If I dont get any response then I would follow up again, I would escalate by reaching out to another channel.
  I would escalate when it affects my works and deadlines.

### What are some things you won't do?
- Saying any harsh words to my teammates and ignore my task, I won’t overwork or sacrifice my well-being, Ignore communication or disappear without notice, and pretend to understand something when I don't.
