# Use of AI Tools

## Research & Learn
## What AI tools are typically used for your role?
- I often use ChatGPT and Gemini in most of my projects.  
## What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?
- The benefits is it can enhance productivity, automation of repetitive tasks, and improved decision-making. However, there are risks involved such as data privacy, potential inaccuracies of AI-generated outputs, and over reliance on AI tools. That's why it's important to use AI responsibly.
## What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?
- Any data that could be the reason of data leak in the company. Example is the API keys or any information in the .env file of a project.
## How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?
- I would verify the information using reliable sources like official websites and documentations. There are multiple sources you can use to check facts. For technical, I would test and validate first, always prove it works properly.


## Reflection
## When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?
- When you're stuck in a certain problem that's when you needed AI or when there's a repetitive task. Rely on your own skills when you know what to do and when handling such sensitive information just to ensure the accuracy and the quality of task.
## How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?
- Always think that AI is a calculator that can assist and support you. Try to solve problem in your own thinking then if you're stuck then that's the time where you need the help of AI.
## What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?
- I would avoid putting sensitive or confidential information into AI systems, in that way I can ensure data privacy.
