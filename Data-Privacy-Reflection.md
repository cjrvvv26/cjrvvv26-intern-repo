# Data Privacy & Confidentiality
## Research & Learn
### What types of data are considered confidential at Focus Bear?
- Identification data, Social data, academic & professional data, commercial & marketing data
  Economic, Financial and Insurance Data, Technical Data, Aggregate Data, Special Category of Personal Data.
### What are best practices for handling confidential data?
- avoiding sharing sensitive data without proper authorization. Also important to follow the
  company policies.
### How should you respond to a suspected data breach or accidental disclosure of confidential information?
- If I suspect a data breach or accidental disclosure,
  I would report it immediately to my supervisor or the appropriate team.
  I would avoid fixing it in my own and I will cooperate with the investigation.

## Reflection
### What steps can you take to ensure you handle data securely in your daily tasks?
- I can ensure the security of data by using strong passwords, keeping my account private, and
  access it when necessary only. Last, by being aware and stay away from phishing attacks.
### How should you store, share, and dispose of sensitive information safely?
- Sensitive information should be stored in secure and password protected systems. When no longer needed,
  it should be properly deleted or disposed of following company guidelines.
### What are some common mistakes that lead to data privacy issues, and how can they be avoided?
- Weak passwords, sharing data to those unauthorized people, falling for phishing attacks, and
  not following company policies. They can be avoided by staying aware and cautious, verifying sources,
  and using strong passwords.
