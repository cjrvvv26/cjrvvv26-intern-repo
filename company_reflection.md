# Understanding Focus Bear’s Mission & Vision

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
- I think it was created to help ADHD users to manage their tasks properly, to get more focus while doing their work. The app offers reminders and features to help them establish consistent routines.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?
- Focus Bear addresses problems like difficulty starting tasks, time blindness, and getting distracted easily. For example, the distraction blocker feature prevents access to apps or websites during focus sessions, while the Pomodoro-style timers and scheduled routines help users stay on track without overthinking what to do next.

## Why do you think this mission is important?
- Well it's not just an ordinary productivity tool or app. It address the real neurological challenges that some people experienced and it affects their daily life.

## How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?
- It aligns with my personal interest because, I'm passionate about using technology to solve real world issues. I like that Focus Bear focuses not just on productivity, but on behavior and habit-building, which is more meaningful and long-term compared to simple task trackers.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
- Yes, as a student, I sometimes struggle with procrastination and getting started on tasks. A feature I would personally like to use is the morning routine, because I often feel unorganized at the start of my day. I think this feature could help me begin my day with clear steps and build a consistent habit. The distraction blocker would also help me avoid opening social media while studying, which is something I struggle with.
