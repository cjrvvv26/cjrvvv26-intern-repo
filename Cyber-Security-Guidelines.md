## Cyber Security

## Research & Learn

* What are common cyber security threats in a remote work environment?
- Phishing Attacks, Malware or Malicious Software, Denial of Service, Data Breaches, Password Attacks

* What are best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?
- Must have a strong password, Enable 2FA, Be aware of phishing, limit what you share online, and keep your software updated.

* Why is it important to lock your computer when away from your desk?
- You can secure your data or information by locking your computer. It prevents malicious acts under your name.

* How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?
- By staying aware of common attacker methods and taking careful steps before interacting with any links or messages.

* What makes a strong password, and why should you use a password manager?
- Strong password consists a complex, long, and with special characters. Password manager helps you stay secure by storing your password in an encrypted storage and making it convinient for you because it has auto-filling logins.

* Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important, and when should you enable it?
- It's an extra layer of security beyond your password. When someone trying to access into your account, they needed to have a second factor like a code from your phone. It prevents unauthorized access even if your password compromised.
